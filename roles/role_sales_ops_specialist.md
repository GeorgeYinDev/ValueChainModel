# Role Definition: Sales Operations Specialist

- **Role ID**: `role_sales_ops_specialist`
- **Department**: Commercial Operations & Revenue Ops
- **Reports To**: Sales Operations Director
- **Internal / External**: Internal

## Key Responsibilities
- Review customer purchase orders for pricing terms, catalog configurations, and contract compliance.
- Capture and validate sales orders in the CRM / CPQ system and dispatch to ERP.
- Monitor order fulfillment progress and communicate delivery milestones with account executives.
- Coordinate order amendment exceptions and return merchandise authorizations (RMAs).

## Decision Rights & Financial Approval Limits
- **Discretionary Order Discount Authority**: Up to 10% off list price.
- **Order Cancellation Authority**: Unreleased backorders only.

## Required IT Systems Access
- `asset_crm_system` (Role: CPQ Administrator)
- `asset_erp_system` (Role: Sales Order Entry Processor)

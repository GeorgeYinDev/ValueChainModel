# Role Definition: General Ledger Accountant

- **Role ID**: `role_general_ledger_accountant`
- **Department**: Corporate Controller & General Accounting
- **Reports To**: Finance Controller
- **Internal / External**: Internal

## Key Responsibilities
- Record and post recurring, accrual, and non-standard manual journal entries with required supporting documentation.
- Ingest and substantiate feeder subledger activity across Accounts Payable (AP), Accounts Receivable (AR), Fixed Assets (FA), and Payroll.
- Execute month-end balance sheet account reconciliations, variance investigations, and variance explanations.
- Maintain corporate chart of accounts (COA) mapping and cost center allocation tables.

## Decision Rights & Financial Approval Limits
- **Journal Entry Preparation Limit**: Unlimited preparation; posting restricted according to materiality thresholds.
- **Direct Journal Posting Limit**: Up to $10,000 USD for standard recurring/clearing items (entries >= $10,000 USD require Controller sign-off).
- **Subledger Reconciliation Sign-off**: Primary Preparer sign-off on balance sheet accounts.

## Required IT Systems Access
- `asset_erp_system` (Role: General Ledger Accountant / Journal Entry Preparer)
- `asset_financial_consolidation_system` (Role: Local Entity Financial Submitter)

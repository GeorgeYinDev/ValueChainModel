# Role Definition: Supplier / Vendor

- **Role ID**: `role_supplier`
- **Department**: External Enterprise Partner
- **Reports To**: N/A (External Commercial Entity)
- **Internal / External**: External

## Key Responsibilities
- Submit bids and commercial proposals during RFx sourcing events.
- Acknowledge Purchase Orders and commit to delivery timelines.
- Ship goods or deliver services accompanied by Advanced Shipping Notices (ASNs) and delivery dockets.
- Submit accurate tax invoices via the e-Procurement supplier portal.

## Decision Rights & Financial Approval Limits
- **Contract Acceptance**: Authorized representative signature on commercial agreements.

## Required IT Systems Access
- `asset_eprocurement_portal` (Role: Vendor Portal Self-Service User)

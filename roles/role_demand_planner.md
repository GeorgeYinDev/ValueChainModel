# Role Definition: Demand Planner

- **Role ID**: `role_demand_planner`
- **Department**: Supply Chain Planning
- **Reports To**: VP of Supply Chain
- **Internal / External**: Internal

## Key Responsibilities
- Analyzing market trends, historical sales data, and promotional inputs to generate the statistical demand forecast.
- Collaborating with Sales and Marketing in S&OP (Sales & Operations Planning) meetings to align on consensus demand.

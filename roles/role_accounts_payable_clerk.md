# Role Definition: Accounts Payable Clerk

- **Role ID**: `role_accounts_payable_clerk`
- **Department**: Accounts Payable / Global Business Services
- **Reports To**: AP Operations Manager
- **Internal / External**: Internal

## Key Responsibilities
- Ingest and index incoming supplier invoices (EDI, OCR, manual entry).
- Perform 3-way matching against Purchase Orders and Goods Receipts (GRs).
- Investigate and resolve quantity, price, or tax matching exceptions.
- Prepare voucher payment proposals for Treasury disbursement approval.

## Decision Rights & Financial Approval Limits
- **Invoice Exception Override Limit**: Up to $1,000 USD variance tolerance.
- **Payment Voucher Creation**: Responsible for voucher batch preparation.

## Required IT Systems Access
- `asset_erp_system` (Role: Accounts Payable Accountant)
- `asset_eprocurement_portal` (Role: Invoice Processor)

# Role Definition: Financial Consolidation Specialist

- **Role ID**: `role_consolidation_specialist`
- **Department**: Corporate Financial Reporting & Consolidation
- **Reports To**: Finance Controller
- **Internal / External**: Internal

## Key Responsibilities
- Orchestrate multi-entity intercompany transaction matching, dispute resolution, and cross-border elimination entries.
- Perform foreign currency revaluation, translation (CTA), and cumulative translation adjustments across global subsidiaries.
- Execute group consolidation runs, minority interest calculations, and equity pickup roll-ups.
- Author external consolidated financial statements, 10-K/10-Q disclosures, footnotes, and executive management reporting decks.

## Decision Rights & Financial Approval Limits
- **Intercompany Matching Tolerance Discretion**: Resolve intercompany un-reconciled breaks under $5,000 USD via plug accounts.
- **Consolidation Elimination Posting**: Execute automated and top-side group elimination journal batches.
- **Reporting Package Release Authority**: Technical sign-off on consolidated preliminary financial trial balances.

## Required IT Systems Access
- `asset_erp_system` (Role: Enterprise Group Consolidation Analyst)
- `asset_financial_consolidation_system` (Role: Group Consolidation Administrator & Power User)

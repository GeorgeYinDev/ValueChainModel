# Role Definition: Billing & Accounts Receivable Specialist

- **Role ID**: `role_billing_specialist`
- **Department**: Finance & Revenue Accounting
- **Reports To**: Finance Controller
- **Internal / External**: Internal

## Key Responsibilities
- Generate customer billing documents and tax invoices immediately following proof of delivery.
- Ensure automated EDI 810 invoice dispatch and portal distribution to client AP departments.
- Match incoming customer payments (wire, ACH, card) against open AR receivables.
- Investigate short-payments, billing disputes, and apply credit memos when authorized.

## Decision Rights & Financial Approval Limits
- **Credit Memo Issuance Threshold**: Up to $5,000 USD (higher amounts require Controller approval).
- **Payment Write-off Limit**: Up to $100 USD for rounding/currency conversion variance.

## Required IT Systems Access
- `asset_erp_system` (Role: Accounts Receivable Accountant)
- `asset_payment_gateway` (Role: Inbound Settlement Reconciliation Auditor)

# Role Definition: Inventory Manager

- **Role ID**: `role_inventory_manager`
- **Department**: Warehouse & Logistics
- **Reports To**: Supply Chain Director
- **Internal / External**: Internal

## Key Responsibilities
- Ensuring accurate cycle counts and systemic inventory accuracy across all storage bins.
- Managing the put-away of finished goods from the factory floor and setting ATP (Available to Promise) limits for sales.

## Required IT Systems Access
- `asset_wms_system` (Role: Inventory Controller)
- `asset_erp_system` (Role: Materials Manager)

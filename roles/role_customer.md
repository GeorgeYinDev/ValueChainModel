# Role Definition: Enterprise Customer

- **Role ID**: `role_customer`
- **Department**: External Client Procurement & AP
- **Reports To**: Client Executive Management
- **Internal / External**: External

## Key Responsibilities
- Submit formal Purchase Orders and requests for quotation (RFQs).
- Acknowledge delivery of physical goods or sign off on services rendered milestones.
- Receive customer tax invoices and process payment remittance according to contracted payment terms.
- Raise billing discrepancies or damage claims within contractual notification windows.

## Decision Rights & Financial Approval Limits
- **Contractual Ordering Authority**: Bound by corporate master sales agreement (MSA).
- **Payment Remittance**: Contractual net terms (e.g. Net 30 / Net 60).

## Required IT Systems Access
- `asset_crm_system` (Role: Customer Self-Service Portal User)
- `asset_payment_gateway` (Role: Payment Settlement Remitter)

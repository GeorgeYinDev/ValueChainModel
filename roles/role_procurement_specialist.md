# Role Definition: Procurement Specialist

- **Role ID**: `role_procurement_specialist`
- **Department**: Operational Procurement
- **Reports To**: Procurement Manager
- **Internal / External**: Internal

## Key Responsibilities
- Review purchase requisitions for catalog compliance and spending limit validation.
- Convert approved purchase requisitions into formal Purchase Orders (POs).
- Monitor PO dispatch and supplier acknowledgment status.
- Resolve order delivery exceptions and liaise between requisitioners and vendors.

## Decision Rights & Financial Approval Limits
- **PO Approval Threshold**: Up to $50,000 USD
- **Contract Sign-off Limit**: Non-binding catalog purchases only.

## Required IT Systems Access
- `asset_eprocurement_portal` (Role: Buyer)
- `asset_erp_system` (Role: PO Processor)

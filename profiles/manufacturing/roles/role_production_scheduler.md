# Role Definition: Production Scheduler

- **Role ID**: `role_production_scheduler`
- **Department**: Manufacturing
- **Reports To**: Plant Manager
- **Internal / External**: Internal

## Key Responsibilities
- Translating the Master Production Schedule (MPS) into detailed, finite-capacity shop floor schedules.
- Sequencing production orders to minimize changeover times and optimize machine utilization.

## Required IT Systems Access
- `asset_erp_system` (Role: Production Planner)
- `asset_mes_system` (Role: Scheduler)

# Role Definition: Credit & Risk Manager

- **Role ID**: `role_credit_manager`
- **Department**: Treasury & Commercial Risk
- **Reports To**: Finance Controller
- **Internal / External**: Internal

## Key Responsibilities
- Establish customer credit lines, conduct Dun & Bradstreet risk assessments, and maintain credit ratings.
- Review order hold exceptions triggered by credit limit breaches or overdue receivables.
- Authorize temporary credit limit overrides or require prepayment/letters of credit (LC).
- Coordinate with collections and legal for delinquent accounts.

## Decision Rights & Financial Approval Limits
- **Credit Line Underwriting Limit**: Up to $500,000 USD.
- **Credit Hold Release Authority**: Orders up to $250,000 USD.

## Required IT Systems Access
- `asset_erp_system` (Role: Credit Management & Risk Controller)
- `asset_crm_system` (Role: Customer Master Credit Viewer)

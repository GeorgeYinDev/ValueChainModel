# Role Definition: Category Manager

- **Role ID**: `role_category_manager`
- **Department**: Global Procurement
- **Reports To**: Director of Strategic Sourcing
- **Internal / External**: Internal

## Key Responsibilities
- Formulate category spend strategies and market intelligence benchmarks.
- Oversee RFx sourcing events, supplier evaluations, and award decisions.
- Lead contract and SLA negotiations for high-value supplier agreements.
- Accountable for category cost savings targets and vendor performance reviews.

## Decision Rights & Financial Approval Limits
- **PO Approval Threshold**: Up to $500,000 USD
- **Contract Sign-off Limit**: Up to $2,500,000 USD
- **Sourcing Award Approval**: Accountable signoff on strategic RFx awards.

## Required IT Systems Access
- `asset_eprocurement_portal` (Role: Category Administrator / RFx Manager)
- `asset_erp_system` (Role: Sourcing Buyer / Master Vendor Approver)

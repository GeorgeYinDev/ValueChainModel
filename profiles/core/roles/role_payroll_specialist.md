# Role Definition: Payroll Specialist

- **Role ID**: `role_payroll_specialist`
- **Department**: Finance / Payroll
- **Reports To**: Payroll Manager
- **Internal / External**: Internal

## Key Responsibilities
- Processing bi-weekly and monthly payroll cycles.
- Handling off-cycle manual checks, bonus payouts, and separation settlements.
- Transmitting tax withholdings and generating the GL journal entry.

## Required IT Systems Access
- `asset_payroll_engine` (Role: Payroll Processor)
- `asset_hcm_platform` (Role: Payroll Viewer)
